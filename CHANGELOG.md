# FrameCapt – what's new

Downloads: [Microsoft Store](https://apps.microsoft.com/detail/9NGWMW4C9QJM) (updates automatically) ·
[Releases](https://github.com/MiroslavDivis/FrameCapt-releases/releases) · [framecapt.com](https://www.framecapt.com)

## 1.1.2 – 23 November 2026

- Microsoft Store version: the taskbar icon no longer shows the Windows accent colour in its rounded corners.

## 1.1.1 – 23 November 2026

- Settings → Licence shows the **Computer ID**. Click it to copy the licence details for support
  (end of the key, computer name and ID – never the full key).
- **Transfer via support** button: opens an e-mail to support with these details; without an e-mail
  program they are copied to the clipboard.
- Licences: a computer that was blocked by the licence owner (for example a lost laptop) gets a clear
  message. The number of computers per licence is enforced.

## 1.1.0 – 20 November 2026

- **Straighten** a crooked capture or photo with the lever above it; the crop is shown live.
- **Combine captures**: paste another capture or picture (Ctrl+V or drag a file in), then move, resize,
  rotate it and change the layer order.
- **Edit saved captures again**: reopen a saved image (Ctrl+O, tray → Open image…, Recent captures) and keep
  moving or changing its annotations. Nothing extra is stored next to your file; privacy masks stay permanent.
- **Save as** (Ctrl+S) to any folder, name and format.
- **Up to 10 save folders** (keys 1–9 and 0); the number keys work on every keyboard layout and on the numeric keypad.
- **Keyboard shortcuts** in Settings: the capture-menu shortcut can be changed; Pro adds direct shortcuts for
  region, window, full screen and scrolling capture.
- **Pixel-sharp editor**: at 100 % one capture pixel is one monitor pixel, also with Windows display scaling.
- **Tall scrolling captures** open fitted to the width and scroll with the mouse wheel.
- **Undo a capture**: Ctrl+Z brings back the previous capture – also while selecting a new one; Ctrl+Y returns.
- Unfinished work is offered for restore after FrameCapt was closed, with "Remember my choice".
- Callouts: one continuous outline, drag the tail tip to change direction and length.
- Colour and size changes apply to the selected annotation; clearer step numbers.
- Started with Windows: stays in the tray. Tray icon: left click opens the capture-mode window.
- Launcher: "Open image" button. Default folder is now Pictures\FrameCapt.

## 1.0.1 – 15 November 2026

- Scrolling capture works with smooth-scrolling apps (Microsoft Store, WinUI) and sliding sticky bars.
- Time-limited licences show their end date. Full set of taskbar and Start icons.

## 1.0.0 – 14 November 2026

- First release: region, window, full-screen and scrolling capture; annotation editor with arrows, shapes,
  text, numbered steps, callouts and dimensions; privacy masks; save folders; Free / Pro / Enterprise
  editions; 10 languages.
